Squatty Bird

Squatty Bird is an iPhone movement game with five launch levels: Flight School, Moonbounce, Jetstream, Gravity Well and Lava Lift. Each has Beginner, Normal, Hard and Fiendish. Open Instructions on a level card for its mechanic; How to Play explains the selected level and phone setup.

Camera setup and tracking

Allow Camera access, place your iPhone securely in portrait orientation and step back until your whole body and both feet are visible. Use a well-lit, clear space. Stand still for calibration and complete a comfortable practice squat if prompted. Return to standing between squats. Frame Only still requires Camera access.

Levels and permanent unlocks

Flight School is free. Gold is earned with 50 valid squats in one run and unlocks the next level at the same difficulty. All launch levels can be earned through play. Optional individual purchases and Level Pack 1 provide immediate permanent access across all four difficulties without awarding medals. Pack 1 includes the four paid launch levels; its three later included levels arrive through future app updates.

To restore a purchase, open More, choose Level unlocks and tap Restore Purchases while signed into the App Store account used for the purchase. Purchases are managed by Apple. If access is still missing, contact us with the app version and product name; do not send passwords or payment-card details.

Recording, saving and sharing

Arm optional recording before a run. After a recorded run, choose Save to add the video to Photos or Share to choose a destination. A run played with recording off has no video to save. Frame Only uses illustrated scenery with the body outline. For missing Camera or Photos permission, check Squatty Bird in iPhone Settings.

Exercise within your abilities and stop if you feel pain. The app is a game and does not provide medical advice.
