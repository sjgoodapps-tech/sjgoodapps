Design reference

sjgoodapps style guide

Use this guide for changes to the website. The colours and typefaces come from the OneGLP website. All public pages share assets/site.css.

Design approach

Use app names, real app icons, short descriptions and clear links.

  • Use the OneGLP website's dark blue header, white text and teal buttons. Keep the app sections white.
  • Separate sections with fine rules. Keep corners small and leave out decorative shadows.
  • Use the serif for the homepage headline and app names. Use the sans serif for body text and navigation.
  • Use plain headings. Do not add slogans or filler.

Colours

The introduction uses OneGLP's header gradient: #0d1519, #143039 and #18444a. Its text is #eff8f6, with links in #9fe5d7. The navigation background is #0d1d22.

Use --line: #d5dfdb for dividers and --white: #ffffff for app sections and button text. Text colours meet WCAG AA contrast on their backgrounds.

Layout and mobile

  • Use a maximum page width of 1120 px, with gutters from 20 to 64 px.
  • Use spacing in steps of 4 or 8 px. Give main sections 40 to 80 px of space.
  • At 1000 px and below, move the OneGLP links beneath its description.
  • At 720 px and below, stack the hero and app sections. Put the document contents list below the article.
  • At 380 px and below, let the header navigation take a separate row.
  • Use at least 44 px for navigation and action links. Do not make small icons the only way to open a page.
  • Check widths of 320, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 px. Also check keyboard navigation, zoom and reduced motion.

Links and buttons

Use one filled button for the main action in a section. Use text links for other actions. Keep a visible focus outline. External links open in the same tab so the browser Back button works normally.

Writing rules

These are the owner's hard rules. They also apply to future changes and are saved in AGENTS.md.

  • No em dashes. Use full stops, commas or separate sentences.
  • Use simple language. Keep explanations short and direct.
  • No stock explainers such as “the important thing is” or “what I take from it is”.
  • No wording that sounds generated by AI. Remove filler, inflated claims and repeated slogans.
  • No slogans made from a list of verbs. Use a heading such as “Apps by sjgoodapps”.
  • Describe verified app features. Do not invent ratings, awards, testimonials or health outcomes.
  • Use OneGLP as the product name and oneglp.app as its website.
  • Keep the support and privacy wording. Ask the owner before correcting a content error.

Site files

The public pages are index.html, support.html and privacy.html. They use the same stylesheet. This style guide is a design reference and is not part of the customer navigation.

Use relative links and asset paths. The site is hosted beneath /sjgoodapps/ on GitHub Pages. No package install or build step is needed.
